Frequently asked questions

Is this the official DVSA app?

No. UK Theory Test is an independent study aid. It isn't made by or affiliated with the DVSA, and its questions are original. They are not the official DVSA revision question bank. Lessons and questions are written from The Highway Code and public guidance on GOV.UK.

Does it cover the hazard perception test?

The app explains how hazard perception works and is scored (14 clips, 15 hazards, pass mark 44 out of 75), and its Hazard Awareness module teaches you to spot developing hazards. It doesn't include the video clips themselves, so practise those with the official DVSA materials too.

How closely do the mock exams match the real test?

The Full Mock Test uses the real format: 50 questions in 57 minutes, with a pass mark of 43. Questions are drawn from every topic each time. Quick Mock, Smart Practice and the Pick-a-Length Test let you practise in shorter sessions.

I'm learning to ride a motorcycle. Does the app cover that?

Yes. Go to Logbook → Vehicle and choose Motorcycle. Rider questions on CBT, licence categories, helmets and machine checks are then mixed into every exam. Car mode leaves them out.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. Everything works offline, there's no sign-up, and the app has no adverts or tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I change phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, so it isn't synced between devices. It is kept in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so restoring a backup to a new device should bring it with you.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Logbook → Reset all progress and confirm. This clears your miles, streak, plates, completed lessons, sign drill and test history. It can't be undone.

How up to date is the content?

The content reflects The Highway Code and DVSA test format as of 2026, including the 2022 Highway Code changes and the new first-aid questions on CPR and defibrillators. Rules do change, so always check GOV.UK and The Highway Code before your test. If you think something is out of date, please email us.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
